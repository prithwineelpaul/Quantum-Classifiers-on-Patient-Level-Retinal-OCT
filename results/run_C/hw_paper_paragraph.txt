We executed the trained hybrid VQC head on ibm_kingston for 300 class-balanced locked-test images (164 patients). The four 5-qubit sub-circuits were packed into one 20-qubit circuit; after transpilation it contained 109 two-qubit gates (40 logical CNOTs; two-qubit depth 27), median two-qubit error 0.0016027952282073876, median readout error 0.00811767578125. With 1024 shots, hardware macro-F1 was 89.4% (95% CI 85.5-92.8) versus 90.4% for exact simulation (difference -1.0 pp, 95% CI -2.9 to +0.6; patient-clustered McNemar p = 0.26); 97.0% of hardware predictions matched the exact predictions. With 16 shots, macro-F1 was 83.9 +- 1.3%. Measured expectation values were attenuated by a factor 0.75 (r = 0.96), equivalent to uniform per-gate depolarising noise p ~ 0.010 in our simulator. No error mitigation was applied beyond dynamical decoupling.
